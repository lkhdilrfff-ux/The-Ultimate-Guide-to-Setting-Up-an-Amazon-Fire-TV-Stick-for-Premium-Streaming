Part 1: Physical Installation and Initial Setup

Setting up the Amazon Fire TV Stick is a straightforward process that requires only a few minutes. Before beginning, ensure you have your home Wi-Fi network password handy.

Step 1
Connect to Power

Plug the small end of the included USB power cable into the micro-USB port on the side of your Firestick. Plug the other end into the power adapter, and connect the adapter to a functional wall outlet.

*(Note: Avoid plugging the USB cable directly into the TV's USB port, as many TVs do not provide enough consistent voltage, which can cause the Firestick to loop or crash).*

Step 2
Insert into HDMI Port

Plug the Firestick directly into an available HDMI port on your television. If space is tight behind your TV, use the flexible HDMI extender cable included in the box to safely position the device.

Step 3
Select the Input Source

Turn on your television and use your TV remote to select the corresponding HDMI input channel (e.g., HDMI 1, HDMI 2) where the Firestick is connected.

Step 4
Pair the Remote and Connect to Wi-Fi

Insert the included AAA batteries into your Firestick remote. Follow the on-screen prompts to pair the remote automatically, select your language, and log into your home Wi-Fi network.

Step 5
Register Your Device

Sign in with your existing Amazon account, or follow the prompts to create a new one. Once authenticated, your Firestick will perform a quick automated check for the latest software updates.
